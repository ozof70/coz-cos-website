Frequently Asked Questions

Your guide to our services, bookings and official channels.

Can a complete beginner ask about a cosplay experience?

Yes. If this is your first time trying cosplay, message COz Cos with your favorite character, preferred date and the makeup, costume or photography help you need. We will confirm the available services and a quote based on your request.

How can I ask about an all-in-one cosplay service?

COz Cos offers cosplay experiences combining character makeup, costumes and photography. Send character references, your preferred date and requirements through Instagram. Confirm the contents and fees for each service before booking.

How should I prepare for my first cosplay photo experience?

Collect reference images of your character and tell us whether you already have a costume or wig and what photographic style you like. Ask about sizing, rental options and makeup in advance. If you need posing guidance, ask about it and confirm the arrangements before booking.

What does COz Cos do?

COz Cos offers complete cosplay experiences covering makeup, costumes and photography, as well as costume rentals and sales, in-person cosplay makeup lessons, makeup services in northern and central Taiwan, Xianyu and Taobao purchasing assistance, and cosplay essentials.

Where are the service locations?

Our service locations are near Yingge Train Station in New Taipei and Nantun MRT Station in Taichung. Please message our official Instagram to confirm the exact location and service arrangements.

How can I ask about a booking or lesson?

Message @cozcos_cosplay on Instagram with the service you want, your character, preferred date and region to ask about availability and a quote.

How do I confirm prices and availability?

See our service price list for package and individual service prices. Confirm costumes, student eligibility, other requirements and availability through our official Instagram before booking.

What is the Shared Closet?

COz Cos Shared Closet is a cosplay costume rental and sharing platform at closet.cozcos.com. Check the platform and costume owner’s information for sizing, rental periods and other conditions.

Where can I buy cosplay essentials?

Visit the COz Cos Myship shop. Products, stock, shipping and payment details are subject to the information shown and confirmed in that shop.

Can I pay or complete a booking on this website?

This website introduces our brand and services and links to six official channels. Please message Instagram for inquiries and bookings, and use the relevant platform for shopping or rentals.

Which social accounts are official?

Our Instagram and Threads handle is @cozcos_cosplay, and our Facebook page is named COz Cos. Use the official links provided on this website.
