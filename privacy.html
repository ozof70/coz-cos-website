我們如何了解網站使用情況

COz Cos 在你允許後使用 Google Analytics 4，了解網站瀏覽次數、使用者數與造訪來源，以改善內容與服務介紹。Google Analytics 會使用 Cookie 識別瀏覽器，並處理頁面、裝置、概略地區及互動資料。官網不會主動將姓名、電子郵件或私訊內容傳送至 Analytics。

你可以拒絕統計，仍可正常瀏覽官網與使用五個平台入口。本機及 Netlify 預覽網址不啟用統計。

你的選擇

Privacy & website analytics

With your permission, COz Cos uses Google Analytics 4 cookies to understand page views, users and visit sources. Analytics processes page, device, approximate location and interaction information. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses or private messages to Analytics. You can decline and still use the website. Local and Netlify previews do not collect analytics.

The buttons above change your choice for this browser once tracking is enabled on the live site. Disabling stops future collection; it does not delete previously processed data. External platforms have their own privacy policies. Contact our official Instagram for questions.
