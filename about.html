About Us

From imagining a character to telling your story.

What we do

COz Cos is a service brand centered on cosplay character experiences. From costumes and makeup to photography, we help you prepare your favorite character and tell your own story.

Trying cosplay for the first time?

Beginners are welcome to ask about a cosplay experience. Message us with your favorite character, whether you already have a costume, and the makeup and photography help you need. We will confirm service details and a quote with you.

Our services

We offer complete cosplay experiences, costume rentals and sales, in-person cosplay makeup lessons, makeup services in northern and central Taiwan, Xianyu and Taobao purchasing assistance, and cosplay essentials. Confirm service details, availability and quotes through our official Instagram.

Service locations

Near Yingge Train Station in New Taipei and Nantun MRT Station in Taichung. Message us about your preferred area and date; the exact address and arrangements are confirmed when discussing a booking.

Shared Closet and shop

The Shared Closet provides cosplay costume rental and sharing information. Our Myship shop offers cosplay essentials. Confirm product, rental and transaction details on the relevant platform.

Official channels

For bookings and inquiries, message Instagram @cozcos_cosplay. Find brand updates on Facebook COz Cos and Threads @cozcos_cosplay.
