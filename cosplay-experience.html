All-in-one cosplay: makeup, costume and photos in one package

All-in-one cosplay puts character makeup, a wig, a costume and photography in one package, so you do not have to book each one separately. COz Cos offers 5 packages, with Basic at NT$4,299 and Student at NT$3,999, in Yingge, New Taipei and Nantun, Taichung. First-timers are welcome.

Do not need a full package? COz Cos also offers makeup services, makeup lessons, photography and self-shoot studios, and costume rentals and sales, so you can choose only what you need. See all services and prices

What does an all-in-one cosplay experience include?

COz Cos experience packages combine character makeup, a wig, a costume and photography. Start by confirming your character, costume size and preferred date, then choose a Basic, Student, Studio Plus, Deluxe or On-location package. The table below lists the photography and deliverables for each option. Confirm character and date availability through Instagram before booking.

How the all-in-one experience works

  1. Choose a character from the character list (53+ costume entries), or send references for the character you have in mind.
  2. Message us on Instagram with your character, costume size, preferred date and region.
  3. Pick one of 5 packages based on the shooting setup and deliverables.
  4. Get ready: each package includes character makeup, a wig and a costume.
  5. Shoot in a plain studio, themed studio or on location, depending on your package.
  6. Receive the retouched photos, animated image and files listed for your package.

All-in-one or booking each service yourself?

Booking separately means coordinating dates, locations and style across makeup, costume, wig and photography, with separate fees for each. An all-in-one package brings them together, so you start by confirming your character, size and date. If you already own a costume or only need makeup or photos, individual services may suit you better: see the service price list.

Can I try cosplay without any experience?

Yes. COz Cos offers cosplay packages combining character makeup, a wig, a costume and photography. First-time visitors are welcome to ask. Share your anime or game character and preferred date so we can confirm what can be arranged.

What should I prepare before asking?

Prepare your character’s name, series and reference images, preferred date and region. Tell us if you already have a costume or wig. If you would like posing guidance or other assistance, ask and confirm availability before booking.

How do I choose a character and costume size?

Browse the character list by series and costume size. The list is a reference, not a guarantee of stock on your preferred date. Confirm fit, costume and wig condition, and availability through Instagram.

Where can I ask for an experience?

Our service locations are near Yingge Train Station in New Taipei and Nantun MRT Station in Taichung. Confirm the exact address, service location and available dates through Instagram.

Can I ask for makeup, costume rental or lessons separately?

We also offer costume rentals and sales, in-person cosplay makeup lessons, and makeup services in northern and central Taiwan. See the service price list and explore our Shared Closet for rental and sharing information.

How do I ask about my first cosplay experience?

Message our official Instagram @cozcos_cosplay with the details below. Confirm service contents, availability and total fees together before making arrangements. This website does not take payment or complete bookings.
